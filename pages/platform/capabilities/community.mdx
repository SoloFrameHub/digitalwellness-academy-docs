# Community & Engagement

Forum system, meetups, messaging, and peer support with AI moderation.

## Forum System (Flarum Integration)

### Platform
- **Flarum** - Self-hosted PHP forum with JSON:API
- **Communication**: Next.js ↔ Flarum via JSON:API
- **Authentication Bridge**: Lucia users map to Flarum users (synchronized logins)

### Features
- **Discussion Boards** - Organized by clinical topic and pillar
- **Threaded Conversations** - Posts and replies
- **Topic Tagging** - Auto-classified into: anxiety, depression, relationships, medication, coping-strategies, crisis, general-wellness, optimization
- **Upvote/Like System** - Users upvote helpful posts (peer credibility signal)
- **Bookmark Feature** - Save discussions (stored in `forumBookmark` table)
- **Full-Text Search** - Across discussions + posts
- **User Profiles** - Display username, avatar, join date, post count

### AI Moderation
- **Content Moderation**: AI flags potentially harmful content for human review
  - Pro-ana / pro-mia content
  - Self-harm encouragement
  - Violence
  - Spam
- **Distress Monitoring**: All forum posts analyzed by DistilBERT classifier
- **Crisis Posts**: Trigger provider alerts if user has assigned provider
- **Moderation Queue**: Human review of AI-flagged content

### Strategic Value
1. **Retention**: Users who engage in forums have 3-5x higher retention
2. **Improvement Signals**: Forum topics reveal content gaps
   - If 50 posts ask about intrusive thoughts but only 2 mention relevant lesson → lesson needs improvement
3. **Safety Redundancy**: AI moderation + distress classifier catch harmful content
4. **Community Moat**: Active forum creates switching cost (users don't want to leave peer network)

## Forum Safety Controls

### AI Content Moderation
- **Pre-Publishing**: AI scans before post goes live
- **Harmful Content Detection**:
  - Self-harm promotion
  - Pro-eating disorder content
  - Violence
  - Spam/scams
- **Human Review Queue**: Flagged content reviewed by moderators

### Distress Classifier Integration
- **All Posts Scanned**: DistilBERT analyzes every forum post
- **Crisis Detection**: Same thresholds as journal/assessment (≥85% confidence)
- **Provider Alerts**: If user has provider, they're notified of crisis posts
- **Crisis Resources**: Crisis-level posts trigger modal with 988 hotline

### Topic Routing
- **AI Classification**: "needs-provider" vs "community-handles"
- **Purpose**: Identify discussions requiring professional intervention
- **Escalation**: Auto-flag for provider review when appropriate

### Post Deletion
- **Moderator Control**: Can remove harmful content
- **Audit Trail**: Deletion logged with reason
- **User Notification**: Author notified of removal with explanation

## Engagement Metrics
