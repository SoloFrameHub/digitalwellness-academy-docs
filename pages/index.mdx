# Digital Wellness Academy Documentation

Welcome to the comprehensive documentation for Digital Wellness Academy — a HIPAA-compliant mental health education platform combining clinical treatment support with peak performance optimization.

## Platform Overview

**Digital Wellness Academy** is a B2B2C practice licensing platform that serves:
- **Psychiatric practices** extending patient care between clinical sessions
- **Corporate wellness programs** providing preventive mental health education
- **Individual providers** offering structured education with clinical oversight

### Core Capabilities

- **30+ Evidence-Based Courses** across therapeutic (21+) and optimization (19) schools
- **560+ Interactive Lessons** with 2,800+ clinical-grade components
- **AI Safety System** with custom DistilBERT distress classifier
- **Provider Coordination** with assignment engine, session prep, crisis alerts
- **Community & Forums** with AI moderation and peer support
- **Analytics Moat** driving continuous course improvement
