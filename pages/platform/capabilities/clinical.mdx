# Clinical Features

Complete therapeutic education system with evidence-based content, interactive components, and standardized assessments.

## Content Library

### Therapeutic School Structure
- **Anxiety & Fear Management** - 5 courses, 40 lessons
- **Mood & Emotional Health** - 4 courses, 32 lessons  
- **Sleep Recovery** - 2 courses, 20 lessons
- **Nutrition & Gut Brain** - 5 courses, 40 lessons
- **Stress & Resilience** - 4 courses, 32 lessons

**Total**: 21+ courses, 380+ lessons, all in MDX format with embedded interactivity

### Evidence Badges
Each course labeled with:
- Clinical framework (CBT, DBT, EMDR, mindfulness, motivational interviewing)
- Guideline source (NICE 2024, Cochrane, APA, WHO)
- Research backing (peer-reviewed studies cited)

## Standardized Assessments (20+ Types)

### Depression & Mood
- **PHQ-9** (Patient Health Questionnaire, 0-27 scale)
  - Item 9 triggers crisis modal if ≥2 (suicidal ideation)
  - Used for baseline and outcome tracking
- **Bipolar Mood Check** (MDQ, 7-item binary screening)
- **Depression Checklist** (custom severity tracking)

### Anxiety
- **GAD-7** (Generalized Anxiety Disorder, 0-21 scale)
- **SPIN** (Social Phobia Inventory)
- **PDSS-SR** (Panic Disorder Severity Scale)
- **Anxiety Checklist** (custom symptom tracking)

### Trauma & PTSD
- **PCL-5** (PTSD Checklist, 0-80 scale)
- **Trauma Response Assessment** (custom)

### Sleep
- **PSQI** (Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, 0-21 scale)
- **Sleep Quality Assessment** (custom)

### Substance Use
- **AUDIT** (Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test, 0-40 scale)

### Topic-Specific
- Burnout Assessment
- Perfectionism Assessment
- Grief Assessment
- OCD Symptom Tracker
- Self-Esteem Assessment
- Eating Awareness Assessment
- Emotional Dysregulation Assessment
- Stress Assessment

### Assessment Features
- **Normalized Scoring** - 0-100 scale for cross-assessment comparison
- **Severity Classifications** - none | mild | moderate | severe | very-severe
- **Assessment History Tracking** - Multiple completions over time
- **Delta Calculation** - Pre/post comparison for outcomes measurement
- **Visual Trend Charts** - Graphical representation of score changes
- **Auto-Migration** - Lazy computation from symptoms (non-blocking)
- **Crisis Detection Integration** - PHQ-9 Item 9 ≥2 triggers immediate crisis modal

## Symptom-Based Learning Paths

### Onboarding Flow
1. User self-identifies symptoms (anxiety, depression, panic, insomnia, etc.)
2. System maps symptoms to courses via SYMPTOM_COURSE_MAPPING
3. User routed to specific treatment pathways
4. Courses appear in "My Path" section

### Example Mappings
- "I have panic attacks" → Panic Disorder course
- "I can't sleep" → Sleep Insomnia course  
- "I feel worthless" → Depression course
- "I worry all the time" → GAD course
- "I experienced trauma" → Trauma Recovery course

## Wellness Dimension Scoring

### 5 Wellness Dimensions
1. **Anxiety Management** (0-100 score)
2. **Mood Stability** (0-100 score)
3. **Sleep Quality** (0-100 score)
4. **Stress Resilience** (0-100 score)
5. **Nutrition Awareness** (0-100 score)

### Computation
Based on:
- Symptom severity (from onboarding)
- Assessment results (PHQ-9, GAD-7, etc.)
- Engagement patterns (completion rates, time spent)

### Tiers
- **Thriving** (80-100) - Functioning well in this dimension
- **Growing** (50-79) - Making progress, room for improvement
- **Needs Attention** (<50) - Priority focus area

### Score History
- Snapshots taken every 24 hours
- Capped at 12 snapshots for performance
- Visual trend charts in analytics dashboard
- Used for personalized recommendations

## Personalized Recommendations

### Algorithm
1. **Primary**: Match symptoms to courses (SYMPTOM_COURSE_MAPPING)
2. **Secondary**: Fallback to weak-dimension courses (if wellness score low)
3. **Tertiary**: Uncompleted courses by recency

### Factors Considered
- Symptom severity from onboarding
- Weak wellness dimensions (scores <50)
- Assessment results (PHQ-9, GAD-7)
- Course completion history
- Engagement patterns

## Extended Profile Questionnaire

### Data Collected During Onboarding
- Age range, life stage, living situation
- Current coping strategies
- Unhealthy coping to change
- Therapy history (yes/no, current therapist)
- Known triggers
- Worst time of day
- User reflections:
  - "What does a good day look like for you?"
  - "What's your biggest challenge right now?"
  - "What kind of support are you hoping for?"
  - "Do you notice any patterns?"
- Learning style preferences
- Time commitment availability
- Support network preferences (alone, group, provider)

### Usage
- Informs personalized recommendations
- Helps providers understand patient context
- Used in session prep briefs
- Guides course selection and sequencing
