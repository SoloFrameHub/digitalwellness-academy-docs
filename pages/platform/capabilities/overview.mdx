# Platform Capabilities Overview

Digital Wellness Academy is a HIPAA-compliant mental health education platform combining clinical treatment support with peak performance optimization.

## Quick Stats

- **30+ Courses** across therapeutic and optimization schools
- **560+ Interactive Lessons** in MDX format
- **2,800+ Interactive Components** (5+ per lesson)
- **20+ Standardized Assessments** (PHQ-9, GAD-7, PCL-5, etc.)
- **Custom ML Safety System** (DistilBERT distress classifier)
- **Provider Integration** (assignment engine, alerts, session prep)

## Two-School Architecture

### Therapeutic School
- **5 Clinical Tracks**: Anxiety & Fear, Mood & Emotional Health, Sleep Recovery, Nutrition & Gut Brain, Stress & Resilience
- **21+ Courses** with evidence-based content (CBT, DBT, EMDR, mindfulness)
- **Symptom-based learning paths** - users routed by symptoms
- **Clinical assessments** embedded throughout

### Optimization School  
- **Five Pillars Framework**: Physical Vitality, Social Connection, Purpose & Meaning, Challenge & Growth, Recreational Joy
- **19 Target Courses** for peak performance and preventive wellness
- **For recovered patients** transitioning to thriving
- **For corporate wellness** programs

## Strategic Differentiators

**1. Provider Integration**  
Not just patient-facing - therapists assign courses, track progress, receive alerts. Creates switching cost.

**2. AI Safety with HIPAA Zero-Knowledge**  
Custom DistilBERT model analyzes text without storing it. 95%+ sensitivity, 3% false positive rate.

**3. Analytics Moat**  
Continuous improvement via real-world data. Day 1 problem → Day 2 fix → Day 3 measurement.

**4. Content Atomization**  
One lesson generates 100+ marketing assets (blog posts, emails, social) for practice growth.

**5. Two-School Model**  
Only platform serving full patient lifecycle: clinical treatment → recovery → optimization → thriving.
