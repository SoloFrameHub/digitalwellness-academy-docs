# Clinical Features

Complete therapeutic education system with evidence-based content, interactive components, and standardized assessments.

## Content Library

### Therapeutic School Structure
- Anxiety and Fear Management - Five courses, 40 lessons
- Mood and Emotional Health - Four courses, 32 lessons  
- Sleep Recovery - Two courses, 20 lessons
- Nutrition and Gut Brain - Five courses, 40 lessons
- Stress and Resilience - Four courses, 32 lessons

Total: 21+ courses, 380+ lessons, all in MDX format with embedded interactivity

### Evidence Badges
Each course labeled with:
- Clinical framework (CBT, DBT, EMDR, mindfulness, motivational interviewing)
- Guideline source (NICE 2024, Cochrane, APA, WHO)
- Research backing (peer-reviewed studies cited)

Quality Standard: A+ courses require five or more interactive components per lesson for therapeutic parity with in-person treatment.

## Symptom-Based Learning Paths

### Onboarding Flow
Users self-identify symptoms during onboarding. System maps symptoms to courses via SYMPTOM_COURSE_MAPPING. Users are routed to specific treatment pathways. Courses appear in "My Path" section.

### Example Mappings
- "I have panic attacks" maps to Panic Disorder course
- "I can't sleep" maps to Sleep Insomnia course  
- "I feel worthless" maps to Depression course
- "I worry all the time" maps to GAD course
- "I experienced trauma" maps to Trauma Recovery course

## Wellness Dimension Scoring

### Five Wellness Dimensions
Each dimension scored 0-100:
- Anxiety Management
- Mood Stability
- Sleep Quality
- Stress Resilience
- Nutrition Awareness

### Computation
Based on:
- Symptom severity from onboarding
- Assessment results (PHQ-9, GAD-7, etc.)
- Engagement patterns (completion rates, time spent)

### Tiers
- Thriving (80-100) - Functioning well in this dimension
- Growing (50-79) - Making progress, room for improvement
- Needs Attention (below 50) - Priority focus area

### Score History
- Snapshots taken every 24 hours
- Capped at 12 snapshots for performance
- Visual trend charts in analytics dashboard
- Used for personalized recommendations

## Mood Tracking

### Daily Check-ins
- Mood Rating (1-10 scale)
- Anxiety Level (1-10 scale)
- Sleep Quality (1-10 scale)
- Energy Level (1-10 scale)
- Optional Notes - Free text reflection
- Coping Techniques Used - Track what worked
- Triggers Identified - Pattern recognition

### Features
- Stored in moodEntry table
- Provider can view latest mood data per patient
- Trend visualization with charts
- Integrated with wellness dimension scoring
- Used in session prep briefs for providers

## Personalized Recommendations

### Algorithm
Primary: Match symptoms to courses  
Secondary: Fallback to weak-dimension courses  
Tertiary: Uncompleted courses by recency

### Factors Considered
- Symptom severity from onboarding
- Weak wellness dimensions (scores below 50)
- Assessment results
- Course completion history
- Engagement patterns

## Extended Profile Questionnaire

### Data Collected During Onboarding
- Age range, life stage, living situation
- Current coping strategies
- Unhealthy coping to change
- Therapy history
- Known triggers
- Worst time of day
- User reflections (good day, biggest challenge, support needs, patterns)
- Learning style preferences
- Time commitment availability
- Support network preferences

### Usage
- Informs personalized recommendations
- Helps providers understand patient context
- Used in session prep briefs
